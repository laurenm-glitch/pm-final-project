# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Persona 2: Fleet Dispatcher at a Mid‑Size 3PL
Role: The coordinator responsible for keeping dozens of drivers moving efficiently throughout the day.
Goal: Push real‑time route updates so drivers stay on the fastest possible path.
Friction (Moment of Misery): Route changes take 10–15 minutes to appear in the driver app, forcing dispatchers to rely on WhatsApp as the “real” system.

Why they embody the hook: Slow sync means drivers literally drive the wrong way — a direct hit to delivery speed, customer SLAs, and operational trust.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** They need to keep multiple drivers on multiple routes updated with route information to keep the routes moving efficiently throughout the day
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Drivers don't get route changes in real time in the app (10-15 min lag) so they have to use whatsapp to communicate route changes to drivers in real time
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Whatsapp
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Identify a route issue in the platform
A stop changes, a customer calls, a road closes, or a driver reports a problem.
2. Push the route update in the official system anyway
They still have to update the platform for record keeping and compliance.
3. Switch to WhatsApp to send the “real” update
They open WhatsApp, find the driver, type or voice record the update, sometimes attach a map pin or photo.
4. Hope the driver sees the message immediately
Drivers are mid route, often hands full, in rain, in traffic, or in low signal areas.
5. Manually confirm the driver received and understood the update
Dispatchers often follow up with: “Did you get my message?” “Don’t go to the old stop.” “Turn around — the customer changed the delivery window.”
6. Track multiple drivers across multiple WhatsApp threads
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** WhatsApp becomes the actual routing tool, while the platform becomes a slow database.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** Ops managers already say they’re evaluating leaner competitors. This workaround is proof the system is failing at its core promise: real time routing.
