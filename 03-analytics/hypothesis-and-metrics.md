## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** Drivers don't get route changes in real time in the app (10-15 min lag) so coordinators have to use whatsapp to communicate route changes to drivers in real time
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Reporting is the lowest, and if they aren't using the other features that provide data for the reports, then they aren't using reports because they know the data isn't there.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Fleet coordinator, reduce time for routes to update for drivers so coordinators don't have to use whatsapp
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** If the routes update correctly, then fleet coordinators would be more likely to use compliance and reporting
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** I expect compliance and reporting will go up when routes update reliably
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** If reporting starts to go up then the routes are updating faster and the tool is being used more reliably
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Core dispatch must not drop
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** One month for compliance and reporting to increase at least 1.0
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on the evidence that coordinators are dropping off at the compliance checks, I believe that reducing lag time for routes to update for drivers will result in the coordinators having higher utilization of compliance and reporting by a 1.0 data point increase in CSAT score.  I will protect core dispatch usage and will make a go/no go decision after one month.
